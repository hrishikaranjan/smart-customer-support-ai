ShopEase Warranty Policy (Fictional — for demo purposes only)

1. Standard Warranty Coverage
   - Most electronics sold on ShopEase (headphones, keyboards, hubs, stands)
     include a 12-month limited manufacturer warranty against defects in
     materials and workmanship.
   - Accessories such as cables and cases carry a 90-day warranty.

2. What Is Covered
   - Manufacturing defects, premature hardware failure under normal use,
     and non-functioning components on arrival.

3. What Is Not Covered
   - Accidental damage, water damage, unauthorized repairs or modifications,
     cosmetic wear and tear, and damage from misuse are not covered.

4. Filing a Warranty Claim
   - Customers should contact ShopEase support with their order ID and a
     description of the defect within the warranty period.
   - ShopEase may request photos or a short video demonstrating the issue.
   - Approved claims result in a free repair, replacement, or store credit,
     at ShopEase's discretion.

5. Warranty Claim Timeline
   - Claims are typically reviewed within 3 business days.
   - Approved replacements ship within 5 business days of approval.

6. Extended Warranty
   - An optional extended warranty (add 12 extra months) can be purchased
     at checkout for eligible electronics for a small additional fee.
