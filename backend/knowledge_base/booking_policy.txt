ShopEase Booking & Pre-Order Policy (Fictional — for demo purposes only)

1. Pre-Order Bookings
   - Some upcoming products can be "booked" (pre-ordered) before general
     availability by paying a fully refundable deposit of 10% of the price.
   - The remaining balance is charged automatically when the item ships.

2. Booking Cancellation
   - Pre-order bookings can be cancelled anytime before the item ships for
     a full deposit refund, processed within 3-5 business days.

3. Booking Priority
   - Pre-orders are fulfilled in the order they were booked ("first booked,
     first shipped") once stock becomes available.

4. Delivery Slot Booking
   - For large items (e.g. furniture, appliances), customers can book a
     specific delivery date and 3-hour time window at checkout.
   - Delivery slots can be rescheduled up to 24 hours before the booked
     slot at no charge; rescheduling within 24 hours may incur a small fee.

5. Service Appointment Booking
   - ShopEase Care technicians can be booked for in-home setup of certain
     electronics. Appointments can be booked, rescheduled, or cancelled from
     the "Bookings" section of the customer account.

6. Missed Bookings
   - If a customer misses a booked delivery or appointment without
     rescheduling, a new booking must be made and a re-delivery fee may apply.
