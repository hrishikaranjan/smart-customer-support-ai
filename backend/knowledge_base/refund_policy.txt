ShopEase Refund Policy (Fictional — for demo purposes only)

ShopEase offers refunds on eligible orders under the following conditions:

1. Refund Eligibility
   - Items must be requested for refund within 14 days of delivery.
   - The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include all accessories.
   - Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable.
   - Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout are not eligible for refund.

2. Refund Process
   - Customers can request a refund from the "My Orders" page or by asking
     the ShopEase support assistant.
   - Once approved, refunds are processed within 3-5 business days.
   - Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Bank processing
     times may add an additional 2-7 business days before funds appear.

3. Damaged or Defective Items
   - If an item arrives damaged or defective, customers should report it
     within 48 hours of delivery with photos for a faster refund or replacement.
   - Damaged/defective items are refunded in full, including original shipping cost.

4. Partial Refunds
   - Partial refunds may be issued if an item is returned without original
     packaging or with signs of use, at ShopEase's discretion.

5. Refund Status
   - Refund status can be one of: "Requested", "Under Review", "Approved",
     "Completed", or "Rejected".
   - Customers can check refund status anytime using their order ID.

6. Cancelled Orders
   - If an order is cancelled before it ships, the full amount is refunded
     automatically within 1-2 business days.
