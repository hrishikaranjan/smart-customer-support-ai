ShopEase Cancellation Policy (Fictional — for demo purposes only)

1. Cancelling Before Shipment
   - Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time before their status
     changes to "Shipped".
   - Customers can cancel from the "My Orders" page or by asking the
     ShopEase support assistant with their order ID.
   - Cancelled orders are refunded in full automatically within 1-2
     business days (see Refund Policy).

2. Cancelling After Shipment
   - Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled directly.
   - Customers may refuse delivery, or request a return once the item
     arrives, which follows the standard Refund Policy timelines.

3. Cancelling Part of an Order
   - For multi-item orders, individual items can be cancelled separately
     as long as that specific item has not yet shipped. The rest of the
     order will ship normally.

4. Subscription or Recurring Orders
   - Recurring/subscription orders can be cancelled anytime before the
     next billing date to avoid being charged for the next cycle.
   - Cancelling a subscription does not refund past charges already billed.

5. Cancellation Confirmation
   - A cancellation confirmation email is sent immediately after a
     cancellation request is processed successfully.
