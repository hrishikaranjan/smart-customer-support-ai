ShopEase Frequently Asked Questions (Fictional — for demo purposes only)

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Use your order ID with the ShopEase support assistant, or check the
"My Orders" page. Order statuses include Processing, Shipped, Out for
Delivery, Delivered, and Cancelled.

Q: How do I contact a human agent?
A: Ask the ShopEase support assistant to speak with a human, or say you
are unsatisfied with the automated help. A support ticket will be created
and a human agent will follow up.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: ShopEase accepts all major credit/debit cards, PayPal, and popular
digital wallets. Payment is charged at the time of order confirmation,
except for pre-order deposits (see Booking Policy).

Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?
A: Yes, as long as the order has not yet shipped. Once shipped, the
address can no longer be changed by ShopEase and must be handled with
the courier directly.

Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?
A: Yes, gift wrapping is available at checkout for a flat fee of $2.99
per item.

Q: How do I create an account?
A: You can create a ShopEase account during checkout, or beforehand from
the sign-up page, using your email address.

Q: Is my payment information secure?
A: Yes, ShopEase uses industry-standard encryption and never stores raw
card numbers on its own servers.

Q: What if an item I want is out of stock?
A: You can check current availability using the ShopEase support
assistant, or sign up for a back-in-stock email alert on the product page.

Q: How long does customer support take to respond?
A: The AI assistant responds instantly. Escalated tickets are typically
reviewed by a human agent within a few business hours.
