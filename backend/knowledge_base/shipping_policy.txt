ShopEase Shipping Policy (Fictional — for demo purposes only)

1. Shipping Methods
   - Standard Shipping: 5-7 business days, free on orders over $50.
   - Express Shipping: 2-3 business days, flat rate of $9.99.
   - Overnight Shipping: 1 business day, flat rate of $19.99 (order before 2 PM local time).

2. Order Processing Time
   - Orders are processed within 1-2 business days before shipping.
   - Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.

3. Order Tracking
   - A tracking number is emailed once the order ships.
   - Tracking can also be viewed anytime via order status lookup using the order ID.
   - Order statuses include: "Processing", "Shipped", "Out for Delivery",
     "Delivered", and "Cancelled".

4. International Shipping
   - ShopEase currently ships to over 30 countries.
   - International orders may take 7-21 business days depending on customs.
   - Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.

5. Delayed or Lost Packages
   - If a package is delayed more than 3 days past its estimated delivery
     date, customers should contact support for investigation.
   - If a package is confirmed lost in transit, ShopEase will send a free
     replacement or issue a full refund.

6. Address Changes
   - Shipping addresses can only be changed before an order status changes
     to "Shipped". After that, the customer must contact the courier directly.
